# HOMESTEAD app icon

The player's own artwork (chosen in the dev chat), not the Lucide house style:
the punk-monster mascot. A hot-pink (`#FF6EC7`) horizontal oval body, one big
white eye with a dark pupil left of centre, and three lime (`#B5E853`) spikes
on top, on the app's dark ground (`#0f0d16`).

`icon.svg` is the source; `icon.png` is it rendered at 512 x 512. The browser
tab icon in `public/index.html` draws the same shapes.
